How do I choose my size?

Use your usual size as a starting point. Our pieces are intentionally relaxed; message us on WhatsApp for measurements before ordering.

How long does delivery take?

Lagos delivery usually takes 1-2 business days. Nationwide delivery times vary by location and will be confirmed with your order.

Can I exchange an item?

Yes. Contact us within 48 hours of delivery, with the item unworn and in its original condition, and we will guide you through the exchange.

How do I pay?

Checkout captures your order details first. We then confirm availability and send the approved payment instructions directly.
